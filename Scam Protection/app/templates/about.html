How ScamShield Works

=======

💡 How ScamShield Works

>>>>>>> afb78ac4cb68de80270c30277448f314c4289cf6

Detection Signals (8 Features)

  1. Call Active: Whether a phone call is ongoing during payment
  2. Remote App Present: Detection of screen-sharing/remote-access apps
  3. Payee is New: Whether the payee was recently added
  4. Payee Age: Days since the payee was created
  5. Amount Ratio: Payment size compared to user's typical amount
  6. Confirmation Time: Time spent on payment confirmation screen
  7. Payee Reports: Previous fraud reports associated with payee
  8. Time of Day: Transaction hour (0-23)

Dual-Engine Risk Assessment

1. Machine Learning Model:

  • Logistic Regression trained on 3,000 synthetic transactions
  • Explainable model with interpretable feature weights
  • Provides probability score for fraud likelihood

2. Transparent Rule-Based Scoring:

  • Weighted scoring system for each risk signal
  • Provides clear, auditable risk calculation
  • Generates human-readable explanations

Four-Tier Response System

  1. PASS (0-24 points): Transaction appears normal
  2. SOFT WARNING (25-44): Some unusual signals detected
  3. DELAY & CONFIRM (45-69): Multiple suspicious indicators
  4. HUMAN REVIEW (70+): High-risk pattern detected

Privacy & Data Protection

🔒 Privacy & Data Protection

What ScamShield Analyzes:

  • Transaction metadata (amount, payee, timing)
  • Session context (call status, app detection)
  • Behavioral signals (confirmation time)

What ScamShield Does NOT:

  • ❌ Record or analyze call audio
  • ❌ Store phone conversations
  • ❌ Use voice cloning or speech recognition

🚀 Real-World Deployment Requirements

For production deployment, ScamShield would need:

  1. Real Training Data: Historical fraud cases with labeled outcomes
  2. Platform Integration: Authorization from OS/banking partners

  8. Bias Auditing: Ensure fair treatment across demographics
